# SCDO Wallet 3.1.12 (2026-10-19)

Agent card on the home screen.

- **x402 Base USDC reserve address.** The address is assigned when the agent registers: the wallet asks for the agent's own address and the SCDO Agent Ledger records it, self-proven by the agent's signature. Until then the card says "Not yet assigned (assigned when the agent registers)" instead of a raw "pending". If the ledger has not returned the address yet, the wallet registers again on a backoff (1, 2, 5, 10, then every 15 minutes). Agents that registered earlier, including 3.1.10 and 3.1.11, get it from the ledger on their next heartbeat.
- **One online state.** The presence line and the Status row both come from the agent's own online state: registered, with a register or heartbeat accepted by the ledger in the last 15 minutes. Online shows a green "Online · keep app running". Offline shows a grey "Offline · goes online when the wallet is open". The card and the tray menu update when the agent goes online or offline. In 3.1.11 the card was read once at startup, so it could show online and offline at the same time.
- **Ledger entries** on the card are shown in the selected language.
- **華語 wording.** Traditional characters throughout (在線, 離線, 請保持程式執行). The older release notes no longer use the word 中文 as a language label.
- The 3.1.11 note no longer says the Mac looks closer to native. That change was not in 3.1.11.
