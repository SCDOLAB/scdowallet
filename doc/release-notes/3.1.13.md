# SCDO Wallet 3.1.13 (2026-10-19)

- **Incoming SCDO notifications.** When SCDO arrives, the wallet shows "Received +X SCDO" with the source: wheel win, bookkeeping reward, interest or transfer. Windows shows a toast with a coin sound and a tray badge; Mac uses Notification Centre. On by default; the sound can be muted in Settings. Bookkeeping rewards are grouped into at most one notification every 10 minutes.
- **Reward destination (Coming soon).** Bookkeeping settings show two new options: auto-join wheel and lend to treasury for interest. Both are disabled and marked Coming soon. They cannot be turned on in this version; they become available only after the services are live.
